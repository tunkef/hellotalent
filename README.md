## The Problem

There are **350 million retail workers worldwide.** They have no career platform.

No LinkedIn for retail. No marketplace where a store manager in Istanbul can be discovered by a brand in London. No coaching infrastructure tailored to retail careers. When a retail worker wants to grow, they send the same CV to dozens of job postings and hope someone calls back.

**Hellotalent exists to change that.**

## What It Does

Hellotalent is a **retail talent marketplace** where workers don't apply — they get discovered.

- Retail workers build rich career profiles with experience, competencies, and career goals
- Employers define what they're looking for through position filters
- An AI matching engine connects the right people automatically
- An AI-powered coaching system helps retail workers develop their careers

**No job postings. No blind applications. Just intelligent matching.**

## Tech Stack

Built deliberately without frameworks — vanilla HTML/CSS/JS served as static files. The backend runs on [Supabase](https://supabase.com) with PostgreSQL, Row-Level Security, Edge Functions, and real-time subscriptions. Email infrastructure via [Resend](https://resend.com). End-to-end testing with [Playwright](https://playwright.dev). Hosted on GitHub Pages with Cloudflare.

## Platform Features

**For Retail Workers**
- Career profile with guided setup
- Competency self-assessment and development paths
- AI-powered interview coaching with guided preparation
- Brand discovery and following
- Get matched with opportunities automatically

**For Employers**
- Position-aware candidate matching with 12-signal scoring
- Live candidate pool with match explanations
- Team management with role-based access
- Direct messaging with read receipts

**For Career Coaches**
- Editorial studio for publishing career content
- Coach profiles integrated into candidate experience
- Content moderation and publishing workflow

## Design

Editorial luxury meets accessible warmth. Turkish-first UI. Clatu-led public-site direction. Minimal premium gate patterns where needed. Dark mode. Mobile-responsive down to 390px.
